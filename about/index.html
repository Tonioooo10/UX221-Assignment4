About This Website

This website was created to express my love of the iconic tv show Breaking Bad. It’s a show about a regular high school chemistry teacher and his family until the main character (Walter White) finds out he has cancer. He spirals as he doesn’t have money for cancer treatment and he has nothing to leave behind to ensure his family will be well off. His brother-in-law is a DEA agent and one day decides to take Walter on a ride along where Walter sees his student Jesse Pinkman escaping the police at a meth bust. Walter sees how much money the meth business makes and it gets stuck in his head. He finds Jesse and gets him to cook meth with him that’s the most chemically pure meth ever created and thats where their long journey begins.
